01Introduction

Rickware - Labs ("we", "us", "our") operates this website, the Launcher application, and our Discord community. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect when you use these services, why we collect it, and how it is stored and protected.

By using our website, our Launcher, or our login and backend systems, you acknowledge and consent to the collection and encrypted storage of the data described in this Policy.

02Data We Collect

We collect different data depending on how you interact with us. To keep this clear, we split it into three groups:

Account data you provide directly:

  • Username
  • Password (stored encrypted, never in plain text)
  • Profile picture
  • Phone number
  • Email address

Data collected automatically through website usage:

  • IPv4 address
  • Browser header information
  • Browser fingerprint
  • Browser timezone
  • Operating system timezone

Data collected through the Launcher application:

  • Hardware ID (HWID)
  • MAC address
  • Operating system username
  • System operating system
  • System operating system version
  • System language
  • BIOS ID

Optional data, only if you choose to link a Discord account:

  • Discord username and display name
  • Discord user ID
  • Related Discord account activity, such as roles, server membership status, XP, and Lab-Coins balance connected to your linked account

03How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above to:

  • Create and secure your account, and let you log in from the website and the Launcher.
  • Deliver, license, and update the products you purchase through the Launcher.
  • Bind your account and licenses to your device to prevent account sharing and account trading.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, exploit attempts, and violations of our Terms of Service, and apply timeouts or bans where necessary.
  • Provide support through our Discord server, tickets, and email.
  • Operate the Lab-Coins, XP, and Lab-Pass systems if you choose to link Discord.
  • Maintain the security and stability of our website, Launcher, and backend systems.

04Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the following:

  • Performance of a contract: processing needed to create your account, deliver products, and provide support.
  • Legitimate interest: processing needed to secure our platform, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce device binding and license terms.
  • Consent: processing of optional data, such as linking your Discord account, which you can choose not to provide.

05Data Security

All security and backend systems used to store and process your data have been built in-house by our own team. These systems have undergone a full-scope penetration test carried out by certified security professionals. Sensitive account data, including your password, is stored in encrypted form.

While we take extensive measures to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials confidential.

06Payment Information

We do not store sensitive information such as your geolocation, home address, or payment card details. All payments and related processing are handled entirely by a verified third-party payment provider, which maintains its own security and compliance standards for this data.

07Data Retention

We retain your account and usage data for as long as your account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms of Service, and maintain security records related to bans, timeouts, and device bindings.

08Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar local storage technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and help us understand basic usage patterns. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect the functionality of the website.

09Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only where necessary to operate our services, specifically with:

  • Our verified third-party payment provider, to process purchases.
  • Discord, if you choose to link your Discord account, subject to Discord's own privacy practices.
  • Authorities or third parties, where required by law or to protect our rights, users, or systems from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

10International Data Transfers

Depending on where you are located and where our infrastructure and third-party providers operate, your data may be processed in a country other than your own. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

11Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legitimate need to retain certain records as described in Section 7.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, open a support ticket on our Discord server or contact us by email using the details in Section 15.

12Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13Data Used for Bans & Device Binding

Website login is not tied to a specific device and can be used from anywhere. The Launcher, however, uses hardware and system identifiers, such as your HWID, MAC address, and BIOS ID, to bind your account and licenses to your device. This exists to prevent account sharing and account trading, since binding every account to its owner and device is the only reliable way to enforce this.

If you change your hardware or operating system, this may temporarily prevent login through the Launcher. Opening a ticket on our Discord server allows our team to reset or reassign your account bindings, and in certain cases, such as a GPU, CPU, or motherboard replacement, you may be asked to provide proof of purchase for the new hardware.

14Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
