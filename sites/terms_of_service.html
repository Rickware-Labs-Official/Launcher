06Lab-Coins & Lab-Pass

Lab-Coins (LC) are our centralized in-platform currency. They can be obtained by purchasing Lab-Coin packs, through the Free or Premium Lab-Pass, through Mystery Chest duplicate compensation, through Discord giveaways or drops, or by converting Discord server XP earned through community activity. Lab-Coins can be spent on licenses, products, and the premium Lab-Pass. Lab-Coin packs themselves cannot be bought with Lab-Coins.

Lab-Coins and XP have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for real currency, and cannot be transferred between accounts except through mechanisms we explicitly provide. We may adjust the ways Lab-Coins are earned, converted, or spent at any time.

The Lab-Pass runs in seasons with a fixed start and end date. Every season has a free reward track and a premium reward track. You earn season XP by completing daily challenges that reset every day at 00:00 UTC; each challenge can be completed once per day. Rewards unlock as you level up and must be claimed while the season is active. Unclaimed rewards and season XP do not carry over to the next season.

  • The premium Lab-Pass and the Lab-Pass Bundle (premium pass plus bonus levels) are paid with Lab-Coins, apply only to the season in which they were bought, and are final purchases.
  • Unlock All and Admin accounts include the premium Lab-Pass automatically.
  • License vouchers earned through the Lab-Pass can be redeemed for one eligible paid product within 30 days and create a time-limited license for the voucher's duration.
  • Reward coupons are single-use, personal, and expire 30 days after they are issued.
  • Some daily challenges are tracked by the Launcher. Faking, scripting, or automating challenge progress is prohibited.

07Mystery Chests & Cosmetics

Mystery Chests contain one random cosmetic item: an animated nameplate, an animated avatar decoration, or a name color. Mystery Chests cannot be bought with real money. They are granted as follows:

  • Every new account receives 1 Mystery Chest once.
  • Unlock All accounts receive 10 Mystery Chests once.
  • Admin accounts can open an unlimited number of Mystery Chests.
  • All other Mystery Chests are earned through the Lab-Pass.

Every roll is decided on our servers using a cryptographically secure random number generator. The website only shows the animation of the result your chest already produced. The current drop rates are published at any time in the Mystery Chests section of your inventory. For name colors they are:

  • Common 46.9%, Uncommon 23.9%, Rare 14.2%, Epic 9.7%, Mythical 4.5%, Ultra Rare 0.7%, Ultra Mythical (animated RGB, limited edition) 0.1%

If a chest produces an item you already own, the duplicate is converted into a small amount of Lab-Coins based on its rarity. We may adjust drop rates, the item pool, and duplicate compensation for future chests; the published rates always reflect the current configuration.

Unlock All and Admin accounts have access to every nameplate, avatar decoration, and name color. Cosmetic items are licensed to your account for personal use only, have no cash value, cannot be traded, sold, or transferred, and may be removed if they were obtained through abuse, a bug, or a chargeback.
